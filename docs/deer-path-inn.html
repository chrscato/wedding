1. Summary

Deer Path Inn sells one all-inclusive package priced per person by entrée, from $150 to $225 before fees. That's the highest base price of the venues so far. A 22% gratuity and 8% sales tax make the real cost 1.30× the menu price, or 1.3176× if the gratuity is also taxed. These figures come from the 2025 package; we don't have a quote yet.

  • Capacity is the deciding issue: Windsor Hall seats 200 with a dance floor. Our 215 planning number doesn't fit as stated, so the 215 and 225 columns only apply if the inn can make a larger layout work.
  • The wedding cake isn't included; the client provides it. Budget for an outside bakery.
  • No rental fee, ceremony fee, F&B minimum or deposit amount is listed. The 4-hour bar, 4 hors d'oeuvres, soup and salad are included, and so are valet parking and bartenders.
  • For comparison, at 215 guests Makray runs $34,907 to $41,719, Royal Melbourne $35,411 to $41,643, Lincolnshire $34,341 to $59,509, and Hyatt Lodge's Classic quote is $49,867.

7. Deposit and guarantees

  • A signed contract and a non-refundable deposit secure the date. The amount isn't stated.
  • The final count and detailed seating chart are due 5 days before the wedding.
  • The 22% gratuity and 8% sales tax apply to all food and beverage. Prices may change without notice.
