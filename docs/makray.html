1. Summary

Makray sells one all-inclusive package priced per person by entrée, from $116.50 to $144 before fees. The 20% service charge and 10% tax make the real cost 1.32× the menu price. These figures are estimates from the published 2026 package; we don't have a quote yet.

  • All scenarios include the Classic bar, 3 hors d'oeuvres and the $1,750 ceremony package. The 50/50 row assumes each guest pays for their own entrée; if Makray charges everyone the filet price, the cost is close to the filet row.
  • For reference, Hyatt Lodge's Classic quote at 215 guests is $49,867, which includes room rental but no ceremony fee.
  • The Saturday F&B minimum of $16,000 is well below either scenario.
  • Final payment must be made by cashier's check or cash.

8. Ceremony and enhancements

Ceremony package, $1,750. It includes a rehearsal led by the on-site coordinators, setup, closing off the ceremony areas, preparing the grounds, and white wood garden chairs. You can hold the ceremony on the patio lawn, by the lake, or indoors. A private patio lawn costs $800 more and closes the restaurant patio; it isn't available for Friday weddings.
