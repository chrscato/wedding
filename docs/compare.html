1. Takeaways

  • Makray and Royal Melbourne are the lowest-cost options, both about $36–43k at 215 guests. Lincolnshire is close at the entry level because its $25,000 minimum barely binds.
  • Hyatt is the most expensive at the entry level ($49,867 for Classic), but it's the only venue priced for 2028 and the only one with a written quote. The others will likely rise by the time we book.
  • Capacity rules some venues in or out. Deer Path Inn seats 200, below our planning number. Royal Melbourne and Lincolnshire's Lakeside Pavilion seat 220, which fits 215 only tightly and 225 not at all. Hyatt and Makray haven't stated a capacity.
  • The cheapest totals aren't fully comparable. Deer Path excludes the cake, Hyatt excludes the ceremony fee, and Lincolnshire's minimum comes from an email rather than its package.
