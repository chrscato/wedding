# Deer Path Inn: 2025 Wedding Package

**Location:** Lake Forest, IL (Windsor Hall)
**Contact:** the package names no contact, address or phone. It refers only to "our Catering Managers."

**Source:** `Wedding Package 2025.pdf` (in this folder, 11 pages). Compiled 2026-10-03. The PDF is a scan with no text layer and was transcribed by hand.

## How pricing works

The package is **all-inclusive, priced per person by the entrée you choose**: $150 to $225 per person, before fees.

- **22% gratuity** on all food & beverage. The package calls it "gratuity", not a service charge.
- **8% sales tax** on all food & beverage
- **All-in multiplier:**
  - **1.30×** if the 8% tax applies to food and beverage only. The package says "Please add 22% gratuity and 8% sales tax to all food and beverage."
  - **1.3176×** if the gratuity is also taxed. **Confirm which applies.**
- **Up to 3 entrées, vegetarian included.** The count of each entrée must be given in advance, and place cards must show each guest's selection. The package doesn't say whether everyone is charged the highest entrée price.
- **2025 pricing,** "subject to change without notice." Our wedding would be 2027 or 2028.
- **Not stated anywhere:** a room rental fee, a ceremony fee, an F&B minimum, or the deposit amount.

## What the package includes

- **Cocktail reception:** 4 butler-passed hors d'oeuvres
- **Dinner:** soup (or a plated appetizer for an upgrade fee), salad and an entrée, with assorted breads and butter, and coffee and tea
- **Wedding cake: provided by the client.** It is *not* included, so budget for an outside bakery.
- **Beverages:** a 4-hour First Level bar, a champagne toast, and a choice of house white and red wine with dinner
- **At no additional charge:** ivory tablecloths and napkins, a dance floor and votive candles. The bartender fee is waived and valet parking is complimentary.
- Menus can be custom-designed with the catering managers.

---

## Venue

- **Windsor Hall:** over 4,500 sq ft, seating **up to 200 guests with a dance floor**. It has oak beams, stucco walls and a fireplace, and divides into 3 rooms, which leaves pre-function space.
- **Ceremony:** an "intimate ceremony" in the English garden is mentioned, but no fee, capacity or rain plan is given.

> ⚠️ **Capacity is below our list.** Windsor Hall seats 200 with a dance floor, which is 15 short of our 215 planning number and 25 short of 225. This venue works only if the list comes down to about 200, or if the inn can seat more another way.

## Questions to ask

1. **Capacity:** Windsor Hall seats 200 with a dance floor. Is there any layout for 215–225 (pre-function rooms, a different floor plan)? What's the maximum for a seated dinner?
2. What are the current **2027/2028 per-person prices**? These are 2025.
3. Is there a **room rental fee, ceremony fee or F&B minimum**? How much is the deposit, and what's the payment schedule?
4. Is the 8% tax applied to the 22% gratuity? Does all of the gratuity go to staff?
5. With 2–3 entrées, does each guest pay for their own entrée, or does everyone pay the highest price?
6. **Garden ceremony:** what's the fee, the capacity and the rain plan?
7. What do children's and vendor meals cost?
8. **Cake:** is there a cake-cutting fee for an outside cake? Do you have preferred bakeries?
9. Which 1st Level wines are poured with dinner?
10. Is there a guest room block? What are the rates, and how many rooms does the inn have?
