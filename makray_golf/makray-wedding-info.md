## How pricing works

The package is **all-inclusive, priced per person by the entrée you choose**: $116.50 to $144 per person, before fees.

- **20% service charge** on all food & beverage
- **10% tax** on food, beverage and the service charge
- **All-in multiplier = 1.32×** (1.20 × 1.10)
- **Split menu fee:** +$2/pp for two entrée options, +$3/pp for three. This doesn't apply to combination entrées.
- **Ceremony package:** $1,750. A private patio lawn costs an extra $800, except for Friday weddings.
- **2026 pricing.** Prices may rise but "will not exceed a 5% increase."

### Food & beverage minimums (before tax and service)

| Evening | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Friday | $12,000 |
| Saturday | $16,000 |
| Sunday | $8,000 |

- **Peak season (May–Dec):** 5% off Friday receptions, 10% off Sunday receptions
- **Non-peak (Jan–Apr):** 10% off food & beverage Friday–Sunday, plus lower minimums
- No discounts on holiday weekends.

## What the all-inclusive package includes

- An on-site wedding coordinator through planning, the rehearsal and the wedding day
- A wedding suite with champagne
- 3 butler-passed hors d'oeuvres during a 1-hour cocktail reception
- A 4-hour open bar: the Classic package, plus domestic beers and CK Mondavi wines
- Unlimited red and white wine during dinner
- A champagne toast for all guests
- A 3-course dinner: soup *or* salad, an entrée, and wedding cake with coffee
- A custom tiered cake from Lovin' Oven Cakery, with buttercream icing and filling. A serving set and stand are available on request.
- Table décor: a circular mirror tile and 3 votives
- Linens: white or ivory floor-length linen and colored napkins. The place card, head, DJ, gift and cake tables get floor-length skirting with swag.
- Photos on the golf course: reserved carts and a guided tour for the wedding party
- A private tasting for up to 6 people
- A golf package for 4 with carts. Extra golfers pay standard rates.
- Coat check (seasonal)

- **Mixed entrées:** with a split menu, is everyone charged the price of the most expensive entrée, or is each guest charged for their own choice?

- **Capacity:** what's the ballroom's capacity for 225 seated guests with a dance floor?
