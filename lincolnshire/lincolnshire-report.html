1. Summary

Lincolnshire Marriott sells three packages priced per person by entrée, from $107 to $157 before fees. A 25% taxable service charge and 9.25% tax make the real cost 1.3656× the menu price. When more than one entrée is offered, everyone pays the highest-priced one. The Lakeside Pavilion has a $25,000 F&B minimum for 2027 and no rental fee.

  • All scenarios include the $200 DJ power box and leave out the ceremony ($12/pp), since we're not holding it there. The top row's food and beverage falls short of $25,000 at every count up to about 233 guests, so it's billed at the minimum and doesn't change with the guest count.
  • Because of the minimum, upgrading from Rustic Glamour to Handcrafted Romance costs only about $200 all-in at 215 guests. That buys a 4th hors d'oeuvre, the Top Shelf bar and better linens.
  • Capacity: the Lakeside Pavilion seats 220. A 225-guest list doesn't fit as stated, and 215 fills 98% of it before the dance floor. The Grand Ballroom and Grand Marquee fit, but their minimums are unknown.
  • For comparison, at 215 guests Makray runs $34,907 to $41,719, Royal Melbourne $35,411 to $41,643, and Hyatt Lodge's Classic quote is $49,867.

Method: the entrée price comes from the chosen package column, either the highest price offered or a share-weighted average. Adults pay that plus the per-person add-ons and extra bar hours.
