## How pricing works

Pricing is **per person, by package and entrée**: $107 to $157 per person, before fees. There are three packages (Rustic Glamour, Handcrafted Romance, Genuine Indulgence). The entrée price covers the cocktail hour, dinner, cake and the 4-hour bar.

- **25% service charge.** The menu calls it a "taxable service charge".
- **9.25% sales tax** on food, beverage and the service charge. That's the rate in every menu-page footer. The Hotel Information page (p. 22) says **9%**.
- **All-in multiplier = 1.365625×** (1.25 × 1.0925). At 9% tax it's 1.3625×.
- **Several entrées:** "the highest priced entrée prevails." Everyone is charged the most expensive entrée offered.
- **Customized sides:** +$2/pp. By default the same starch and vegetable go with every entrée.
- **Ceremony:** $12/pp with a **$1,000 minimum**. At 215 guests that's $2,580 before fees.
- **No separate venue rental fee.** This is per the Aug. 2026 email, as summarized in the docx. The PDFs don't mention a rental fee either way.
- **Menu prices are for 2026.** They're "subject to an annual increase", and the service charge and tax can change "without notice."

### Food & beverage minimum (before tax and service)

The PDFs say a minimum applies. It depends on the space, the day of the week and the date. Only food and beverage ordered through the hotel counts. **The PDFs don't list any amounts.**
