## Fees & fine print (applies to everything)

- **19% service charge** (paid to staff as gratuity)
- **6% administration fee** (kept by the hotel)
- **Tax:** the guide says 7.5%, applied to both charges. **Tiana's actual quotes use 7.75%, applied to the 6% fee only,** not the 19%.
- **All-in multiplier ≈ 1.332×** the listed food & beverage price, using the quotes' method. The guide's wording would give ≈1.344×.
- **Room rental** carries its own **25% taxable service charge** plus 7.75% tax. This isn't mentioned in the guide.
- **Final guest count is due by 11am, 14 business days before the event.** After that the number can't go down.
- Pricing may change with market conditions.
- **No outside food or beverage.** Only food and drink bought from the hotel may be served.
- **No-shot policy.** Neat drinks aren't served; all liquor is mixed or served on ice.
- **Food & beverage minimum** depends on the date and event space. Ask the sales manager.

**Things to check with Tiana**
- **Quote header:** it says "Cathey Wedding" and the event date reads "January 00, 1900." It looks like a reused template, so get it corrected before anything is signed.
- **Ceremony fee:** the guide lists a $2,000 ceremony fee, but the quote has a $3,000 "Prairie Ballroom Room Rental" instead. Is that the ceremony fee, a reception room fee, or both? And would an on-site ceremony be added on top?
- **Tax treatment:** the quote taxes the admin fee but not the 19% service charge, at 7.75%. The guide says both are taxable at 7.5%. The quote's method is cheaper by about $500 at 215 guests, so confirm it's what the contract will use.
- **Kids and vendors:** all 215 are priced at the full package rate. Children's meals are $34 and vendor meals are $49 before fees, so subtract those people from the package count. The guide doesn't give an age cutoff for the wedding children's meal; its other menus use "12 & under." Also ask whether guests aged 13–20 get a reduced rate, since they can't use the bar.
- **Deposit schedule:** the quote shows no deposit amounts.
- **Not included:** AV, guest room blocks, ready rooms ($1,000 each), late-night stations and upgrades.
