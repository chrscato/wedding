## How pricing works

Pricing is **per person by the entrée you choose**: $120 to $147 per person, before fees. The entrée price covers the cocktail hour, dinner, bar and the other items listed under "What's included".

- **22% service charge** on all food & beverage. The guide calls it a "taxable service charge".
- **8% Illinois sales tax** on food, beverage and the service charge
- **All-in multiplier = 1.3176×** (1.22 × 1.08)
- **Four entrée choices:** +$3 per plate. The guide doesn't mention a surcharge for two or three choices.
- **Soup *and* salad:** +$8 per person. One first course is included.
- **Ceremony set-up fee:** $1,000
- **Food & beverage minimum:** applies, and varies by date. The guide doesn't list the amounts.
- **2027 pricing**, "subject to change without notice."

## What's included

- Exclusive use of the Pre-Function Room, Veranda and Ballroom
- An in-house wedding coordinator
- Coat check (weather permitting), waitstaff, captains, bartenders and maître d'
- A private bridal suite (**additional fee**, amount not listed) and a groom's room (onsite ceremonies)
- **1-hour cocktail reception, indoor or outdoor:** 4 butler-passed hors d'oeuvres
- **Dinner:** a first course, an entrée, wedding cake and coffee service
- A champagne toast, plus red and white wine served with dinner
- **After dinner:** 3 more hours of open bar
- House white or ivory linens

The open bar runs for the cocktail hour plus 3 hours after dinner. The guide doesn't say whether the bar is open during dinner, or which bar package (Standard or Premium) is the default. The Premium package is priced as an "upgrade from Standard," so Standard is presumably included.

---

## Venue details

- **Grand Ballroom:** seats **up to 220 guests**. It has views of the property and French doors to an outdoor veranda overlooking the golf course.
- **Ceremony:** in the ballroom, on the outdoor veranda or in the wedding garden. The set-up fee is $1,000. Rehearsal time is coordinated with the club.
- **Photos:** allowed on the Royal Melbourne grounds.

> At 200–225 guests, the 220-seat ballroom cap is tight. Confirm the seated capacity with your layout (head table, dance floor, DJ).

### Menu tasting
- 2 hours. Sample **2 salads and 6 entrées**.
- **Free for up to 6 guests.** More people can share from the 6 entrées, or add selections for an extra charge. They can also order from the à la carte restaurant menu at entrée prices.
- Booked with the Director of Catering **2–4 months before the wedding**
- Held at Royal Melbourne's restaurant, **Wednesdays and Thursdays at 3:00 pm**

## Booking & payment

- **No date holds.** A date is secured only with a signed contract and a **$1,500 non-refundable deposit**.
- **Final guest count and full payment are due 14 days before the event.** The invoice is generated then, including service charge, tax and other charges.
- **Payment:** personal or certified check, cash, or credit card (**3% fee**). Make checks payable to Royal Melbourne Country Club.

## Questions to ask Megan

1. What is the F&B minimum for our target dates?
2. How much is the bridal suite fee?
3. Is the Standard bar included in the entrée price? Is the bar open during dinner, or only wine?
4. Is 220 the hard cap for a seated dinner with a dance floor? Can the veranda or Pre-Function Room take overflow?
5. Do two or three entrée choices carry a surcharge, or only four?
6. Is the $1,000 ceremony fee subject to the service charge and tax?
7. Are there off-peak or Friday/Sunday discounts?
8. What are the timeline limits (end time, extra-hour pricing beyond the bar)?
