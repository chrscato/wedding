1. Summary

Royal Melbourne prices its package per person by entrée, from $120 to $147 before fees. A 22% taxable service charge and 8% tax make the real cost 1.3176× the menu price. These figures are estimates from the published 2027 guide; we don't have a quote yet.

9. Spaces, ceremony and tasting

  • Grand Ballroom: seats up to 220. It has French doors to an outdoor veranda overlooking the golf course.
  • Ceremony: in the ballroom, on the veranda or in the wedding garden. The set-up fee is $1,000. Rehearsal time is coordinated with the club, and photos are allowed on the grounds.
  • Menu tasting: 2 hours, sampling 2 salads and 6 entrées. It's free for up to 6 people; others can share or order à la carte. Book it 2–4 months out. Tastings are held Wednesdays and Thursdays at 3:00 pm in the club restaurant.
